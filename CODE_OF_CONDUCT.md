# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, experience level,
nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behaviour includes being respectful, accepting constructive criticism and focusing on
what is best for the community. Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, insulting or
derogatory comments, personal or political attacks, and publishing others' private information.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit
or reject contributions that do not comply. Report unacceptable behaviour to contact@doganddev.eu.
All reports are reviewed and handled confidentially.
